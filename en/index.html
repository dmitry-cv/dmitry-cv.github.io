About me

I started in graphic design and print, then moved into interfaces. Three years on game projects taught me to look at a screen through player behavior: what they'll do, why they'll come back, where they'll drop off.

I can get to the bottom of a task even when the client can't put it into words. I work so that developers don't have to ask twice.

How I work

  1. I ask questions until I understand the project's goal and its users.
  2. I propose solutions, not just pictures, including ideas beyond the brief when they pay off.
  3. I go through iterations and reviews with the team and the client.
  4. I see it through to developer handoff.
